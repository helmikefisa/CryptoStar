What CryptoStar includes

The project is built as an end-to-end automated trading system rather than a simple signal generator.

Market Scanner

Scans Binance Futures markets to identify symbols and setups that meet the configured strategy conditions.

Multi-Timeframe Analysis

Uses M5, M15 and H1 market data to evaluate direction, structure and trade quality.

Support & Resistance

Analyzes nearby market levels as part of entry-quality assessment and helps avoid poor entry locations.

Automated Execution

Designed to execute trades automatically through Binance Futures API integration.

Risk & Exit Engine

Handles configured take-profit and stop-loss logic after an entry is opened.

Subscriber Infrastructure

Includes user-facing subscription and notification functionality for distributing analysis and trade opportunities.
